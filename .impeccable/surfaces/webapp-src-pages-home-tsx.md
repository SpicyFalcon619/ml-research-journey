# Surface brief: Home (and the world every other page inherits)

Mode: Persuade on Home (a first-time friend decides to start; a returning learner decides where to continue). Lesson, track, explore and progress pages inherit the world as Read and Operate surfaces.

## Audience, job, action
First-time visitors are beginner friends of the owner; returning visitors are learners resuming. Action: start or continue the journey. Proof on hand: 80 real lessons, captured output, the owner's own path. No learner counts or ratings exist and none are invented.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The whole guide is one rail map. Every track is a coloured line, every lesson a stop, and the learner always sees where they are. It refuses the category default (centred hero, feature columns, card grid, doc-site tree) and the polish-the-old-look path.

OWN-WORLD: The seven existing themes stay as palettes; the five line colours are derived from each theme's own accent by hue rotation, never new palettes. A condensed transit sign face for headings and labels, a humanist sans for reading, Geist Mono for code only. Square-cornered sign plates, 4px radius at most. Lines are 8-10px with round caps, stops are thick-ringed circles, interchanges are larger white-ringed circles. A five-colour stripe under the top bar is the one signature.

STORY: A friend sees a map of a journey, understands it is one learner's real path, and presses Start at the first stop. A returning learner sees their lit stop and the next stop named, with Keep reading as the single primary action.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Condensed headline and two lines of promise at top left, one Start / Keep reading plate, then the serpentine map across the page: Python, then NumPy, Pandas, Classical ML and PyTorch as joined lines, stops filled when done, the next stop ringed and tagged, a legend that isolates one line on hover, and a tick rail showing position in the 80 stops.

FORM: Metro Map (rail diagram), the dice-assigned world, position 5 of 7 on the grounded list; seed: 20cd218603669956.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
